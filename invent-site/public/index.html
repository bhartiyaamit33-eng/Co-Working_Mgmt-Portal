Not a fest.
A founding day.

On 31 January 2014 the Board of Governors of IIT Bombay approved the Desai Sethi Centre for Entrepreneurship. That date is DSSE Day: the school’s foundation day. INVENT is the public face of that anniversary in 2027 — a YC-shaped gathering for people who build companies out of IITB labs, hostels, and stubborn ideas.

DSSE trains founders in public. Since 2014: 4,300+ students through courses, ~950 mentored, ~340 venture teams, 100+ startups registered. The school sits between E-Cell, SINE, and the rest of the IIT Bombay startup stack.

Who should show up

Student founders with a prototype. Researchers stuck at lab-to-market. Alumni who want to mentor for a day. Operators who can write a cheque or open a door. If you only want a selfie with a stage, skip it.

Request for startups

We are biased toward hard problems. Email a 8-line note to support if you are building in one of these.

Climate & energy Hardware, software, or ugly industrial process. India cannot wait for imported climate tech. Health that ships Diagnostics, devices, care ops. BETIC-adjacent or not. Must leave the lab. Deep tech Space, robotics, materials, AI that is not a wrapper. Prefer teams that can fabricate.
